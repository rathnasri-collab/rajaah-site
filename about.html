Profile summary

Sustainable organizational development must be rooted in a genuine understanding of leadership.

Raja Krishnamoorthy is a seasoned Organization Development (OD) consultant with over four decades of experience dedicated to enhancing organizational effectiveness and building transformative leadership. He partners with corporate, institutional and social organizations to design and implement strategies that foster positive work cultures, high-performing teams and sustainable change.

His human-centric philosophy and unique conceptual models give clients clarity and actionable insights for growth.

Integrated organizational view

Raja offers a holistic perspective of the organization as an integrated human system — through its cycles of Performance, Culture and Excellence.

Human-centric change

Sustainable development must be rooted in a genuine understanding of leadership as different from managership, and a deeper appreciation of structure-systems, true engagement and the power of synergy.
